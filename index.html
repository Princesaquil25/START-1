Career exploration for adults

Discover where your transferable skills could take you.

Your experience may open doors to careers you haven’t considered.

Career Pathway Explorer helps adults explore entry-level administrative and office-based careers, including those with little or no previous office experience or formal training in the field.

What are transferable skills?

Transferable skills are abilities you’ve developed through different experiences that can be valuable in other jobs or work environments. For example, assisting customers can develop communication and problem-solving skills, while organizing family schedules, coordinating volunteer activities, or managing daily responsibilities can strengthen planning and organizational skills.

You don’t need previous office experience to have skills that may be useful in an office-based career.

    About Your Results and Privacy

    Your Privacy

    Career Pathway Explorer does not ask for personal identifying, health, or financial information. Your questionnaire answers are processed locally in your browser and are not saved or transmitted to a server. Your answers remain available until you restart, refresh, or close the page. External resource websites do not receive your questionnaire answers.

    Understanding Your Results

    Career suggestions are based on matching rules developed for this project, not a validated career assessment. They highlight potential career opportunities based on your transferable skills and preferences but do not guarantee employment or eligibility for a particular position.

    Job requirements vary, so review actual job postings and employer qualifications before making career decisions. Learning resources are optional unless an employer specifies otherwise.

    The Bureau of Labor Statistics and O*NET links support the occupation descriptions, common duties, skills, and typical education information. Resource providers describe their own learning materials.
